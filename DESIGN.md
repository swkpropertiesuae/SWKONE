# SWK ONE — Brand Architecture & Design System

**Aesthetic direction:** *Architectural Emirati Luxury — Deep Emerald (`#0b1a33`), Champagne Gold (`#E0C1A3`), Brushed Bronze (`#B68F70`), Deep Bronze Shadow (`#6F513B`), and App Icon Warm Ivory (`#F3EFE6`).*
Grounded in the official `SWK ONE` Brand Assets package (`SWK_ONE_Brand_Assets`): the Mashrabiya Portal Arch Emblem framing twin Dubai skyline towers and desert dunes wrapped in the `S W K` ribbon, paired with the sculptural `SWK ONE` script logo.

## 3. Typography & Layout Principles
- **Display:** *Clash Display* (`600`–`700`) for architectural headers, KPI numerals, and workspace hero titles.
- **UI & Body:** *General Sans* / *Plus Jakarta Sans* (`400`–`700`) with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every AED figure, DLD fee, RERA permit, and Ejari contract number.
- **Arabic:** *Noto Sans Arabic* (`400`, `700`) for bilingual UAE brokerage documents and print packs.
